## Diagram conventions
- Use OCI service names and tenancy/compartment boundaries.
- Show VCN/subnet/route/gateway paths, trust zones, IAM and data flows where applicable.
- Add legends, assumptions, known gaps and operational controls.
- Prefer current Oracle architecture icons when those assets are available and applicable.
- Label conceptual diagrams clearly; do not imply production validation.
