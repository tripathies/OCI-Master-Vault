## Diagram standards
- Use OCI terminology and service boundaries.
- Show tenancy/compartment boundaries, VCN/subnet/routing, identities, data flows and operational controls where relevant.
- Include a legend, trust boundaries and key assumptions.
- Label conceptual diagrams clearly and validate service-specific connections before production use.
